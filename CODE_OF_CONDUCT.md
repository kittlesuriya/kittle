# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We want this to be a welcoming, technically rigorous project. Participants
are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and assume good intent;
- focus criticism on code, decisions, and evidence rather than people;
- welcome different backgrounds and experience levels;
- provide actionable feedback and explain security or compatibility concerns;
- take responsibility for mistakes and correct them constructively.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, sexualized conduct, doxxing, publishing private information, and
deliberate disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove unacceptable content, close discussions,
or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and
productive. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact route in
[`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md) or through the repository maintainer's GitHub
profile.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a
person who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
